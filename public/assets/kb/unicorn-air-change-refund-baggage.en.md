# Unicorn Air Domestic Flights: Change, Refund and Baggage Policy (sold through Unicorn Trip)

> Scope: Unicorn Air domestic tickets (flight numbers `UC1xxx`) bought through Unicorn Trip.
> This is a fictional policy for a workshop; every rule, amount and time limit is sample data.
> Currency: CNY (¥). "Business day" means Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.

## 1. Fare families

**A-01 Fare families**
- Economy: **Lite**, **Standard**, **Flex**;
- Business: **Business**, **Business Flex**.

## 2. Time windows and fee base

Change and refund fees are a percentage of the **fare** (taxes excluded). The window is set by the time between the request and the scheduled departure:

| Window | Time before scheduled departure |
|---|---|
| W1 | 168 hours (7 days) or more |
| W2 | Less than 168 hours and at least 4 hours |
| W3 | Less than 4 hours, or after departure (including no-show) |

## 3. Voluntary change and refund fees

**A-02 Voluntary change fee (share of fare)**

| Fare family | W1 | W2 | W3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lite | 20% | 40% | Not allowed |
| Standard | 5% | 15% | 30% |
| Flex | Free | Free | 10% |
| Business | Free | 10% | 20% |
| Business Flex | Free | Free | Free |

**A-03 Voluntary refund fee (share of fare)**

| Fare family | W1 | W2 | W3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lite | Not refundable (taxes only) | Not refundable (taxes only) | Not refundable (taxes only) |
| Standard | 10% | 25% | 50% |
| Flex | Free | 5% | 10% |
| Business | 5% | 15% | 30% |
| Business Flex | Free | Free | 5% |

**A-04 Minimum fee**
Any change or refund fee that applies is at least **¥50**.

**A-05 Fare difference on a change**
If the new fare is higher than the original, the passenger pays the difference; if it is lower, the difference is not refunded.

**A-06 Taxes**
Each domestic segment carries an airport construction fee of **¥50** and a fuel surcharge of **¥30**. Taxes on an unused segment are refunded in full on a refund, Lite fares included.

**A-07 Worked example**
Standard fare, ¥1,200, refund requested 3 days before departure (W2): fee 25% = ¥300; the passenger gets back ¥900 of fare + ¥80 of taxes, **¥980** in total. A change at the same time costs 15% = ¥180, plus any fare difference.

## 4. Involuntary changes and refunds

**A-08 Involuntary changes and refunds**
If the airline cancels the flight, delays it by **3 hours or more**, or changes its schedule, every fare family can change for free or get a full refund (fare + taxes).

## 5. Other rules

**A-09 Outbound segment not flown**
If the outbound segment is not flown (no-show), the return segment on the same ticket is cancelled automatically, unless the passenger contacts Unicorn Trip **before the outbound departure** to keep it.

**A-10 Name corrections**
Tickets cannot be transferred or changed to another passenger. A misspelt name with **2 characters or fewer** wrong can be corrected free of charge **once**, before departure.

**A-11 Service fee and refund timing**
Unicorn Trip charges no extra service fee for changes or refunds. Refunds go back to the original payment method within **7 business days**.

## 6. Baggage

**A-12 Free checked baggage**

| Fare family | Free checked baggage |
|---|---|
| Lite | 0 kg |
| Standard | 20 kg |
| Flex | 25 kg |
| Business / Business Flex | 30 kg |

Infants under 2: 10 kg free, plus 1 stroller free.

**A-13 Carry-on baggage**
- Economy: 1 piece, up to 7 kg and 20 × 40 × 55 cm, plus 1 small personal item;
- Business: 2 pieces, up to 8 kg each.

**A-14 Buying checked baggage on a Lite fare**
- Online, at least 24 hours before departure: **¥120 per 20 kg**;
- At the airport counter: **¥200 per 20 kg**.

**A-15 Excess baggage and piece limits**
Baggage above the free allowance costs **¥20 per kg**. A single checked piece may weigh up to **32 kg** and measure up to 100 × 60 × 40 cm.
