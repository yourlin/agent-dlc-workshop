# Unicorn Mall Returns, Exchanges and Refunds Policy

> Scope: goods sold by Unicorn Mall (online store and physical stores), part of Unicorn Company.
> This is a fictional policy for a workshop; every rule, amount and time limit is sample data.
> Currency: CNY (¥). "Business day" means Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.

## 1. 7-day no-reason returns

**R-01 Time limit and conditions**
A no-reason return can be requested **within 7 days counted from the day after delivery sign-off**. The item must be unused, with tags, packaging, accessories and gifts complete, and resale must not be affected.

**R-02 Items not eligible for no-reason returns**
The following items cannot be returned without a reason (quality problems are still handled under section 2):

1. Fresh and perishable food;
2. Customised items;
3. Opened cosmetics and personal-care items;
4. Underwear and swimwear whose hygiene seal has been removed;
5. Digital and virtual goods (top-up cards, e-vouchers and similar);
6. Items marked "no 7-day no-reason return" on the product page.

**R-03 Electronics and appliances**
Electronics and appliances qualify for a no-reason return only if the factory seal is intact **and the device has not been activated**.

## 2. Quality problems

**R-04 Handling quality problems**
- Quality problem found **within 15 days** of delivery: return or exchange, at the buyer's choice;
- **Days 16 to 30** after delivery: exchange or repair only, no refund;
- The platform pays all shipping costs for quality problems.

## 3. Return shipping and member benefits

**R-05 Return shipping**
For returns not caused by a quality problem, the buyer pays the return shipping. If the order included return-shipping insurance, up to **¥12** per order is reimbursed to Unicorn Wallet.

**R-06 Free door-to-door pickup**
Gold members and above get **2** free door-to-door pickups per calendar month for returns not caused by a quality problem.

**R-07 Express refund**
For Gold members and above, when the order's paid amount is **¥500 or less**, the refund is issued as soon as the courier collects the return parcel, without waiting for warehouse inspection.

**R-15 Membership tiers**
From lowest to highest: Regular, Silver, Gold, Black Gold.

## 4. Refund timing

**R-08 When refunds arrive**
The warehouse inspects a return **within 2 business days** of receiving it; once it passes, the refund goes back to the original payment method:

| Original payment method | Refund arrives |
|---|---|
| Unicorn Wallet balance | Within 2 hours |
| Debit card / quick-pay | 1–3 business days |
| Credit card | 3–7 business days |
| Cash on delivery | Within 3 business days, to Unicorn Wallet or a bank account named by the customer |

**R-09 Cancelling an order before shipment**
An order cancelled before it ships is refunded in full without inspection; timing as in R-08.

## 5. Stacking discounts and refund calculation

**R-10 Order in which discounts apply**
List price → member price **or** flash-sale price (one of the two) → spend-threshold discount → store coupon → platform coupon.

**R-11 Stacking rules**
- At most **1 store coupon + 1 platform coupon** per order; a spend-threshold discount stacks with both;
- Member price does **not** stack with a flash-sale price;
- Flash-sale items **cannot** use platform coupons.

**R-12 Refund amount for a partial return**
On a partial return, the order's discounts are apportioned across items by their share of the list price; the refund is the item's paid amount after its share of the discount.

> Example: item A ¥200 and item B ¥100 under "¥40 off orders of ¥300", paid ¥260. Returning B: B's share of the discount is 40 × 100 ÷ 300 ≈ ¥13.33, so the refund is **¥86.67**.

**R-13 Returning coupons**
- Full-order return: a coupon still within its validity period is restored; an expired coupon is not reissued;
- Partial return: coupons are not restored.

**R-14 Price protection**
Within **7 days of payment**, if the same item's price drops on Unicorn Mall (flash-sale and live-stream-exclusive prices excluded), the difference is refunded to Unicorn Wallet. Each item in an order can claim this **once**.
