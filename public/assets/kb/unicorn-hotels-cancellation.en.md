# Unicorn Hotels Booking Cancellation and Change Policy (sold through Unicorn Trip)

> Scope: Unicorn Hotels rooms booked through Unicorn Trip.
> This is a fictional policy for a workshop; every rule, amount and time limit is sample data.
> Currency: CNY (¥). All times are the hotel's local time. "Business day" means Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.

## 1. Standard times

Check-in from **14:00**, check-out by **12:00**. "Check-in time" below always means 14:00 on the arrival date.

## 2. Rate types and cancellation rules

**H-01 Rate types**
**Flexible**, **Standard Prepaid**, **Non-refundable Saver**, **Long-stay** (5 or more consecutive nights).

**H-02 Flexible**
Free cancellation or date change until **18:00 on the arrival date**; a later cancellation or a no-show is charged the **first night**.

**H-03 Standard Prepaid**
Free cancellation until **48 hours before check-in time**; a cancellation within 48 hours is charged the **first night** and the rest is refunded.

**H-04 Non-refundable Saver**
No cancellation and no changes; the whole stay is charged.
Exception: if the same Unicorn Trip order contains a Unicorn Air flight that the airline **cancels** (involuntary, see flight policy A-08), the room can be cancelled free of charge.

**H-05 Long-stay**
- Cancelled more than **7 days** before check-in time: free;
- Cancelled between 7 days and 48 hours before check-in time: **1 night** charged;
- Cancelled less than 48 hours before check-in time: **2 nights** charged;
- Early departure: the unused nights are refunded minus a **1-night** penalty.

## 3. No-shows and changes

**H-06 No-show**
For every rate except Non-refundable Saver, a no-show is charged the first night; the remaining nights are cancelled and refunded.

**H-07 Changing dates**
Changing the dates of a Standard Prepaid or Long-stay booking counts as cancelling the original booking and making a new one; the original booking's cancellation windows apply.

## 4. Late check-out

**H-08 Late check-out**
- Check-out between 12:00 and 18:00: **50%** of one night extra;
- Check-out after 18:00: one full night extra;
- Unicorn Hotels **Gold** members: free late check-out until **14:00**.

## 5. Holiday rule

**H-09 National Day holiday**
For bookings arriving between **1 and 7 October**, the free-cancellation limit of the Standard Prepaid rate moves to **72 hours** before check-in time.

## 6. Worked example and refunds

**H-10 Example**
Standard Prepaid, ¥680 per night, 3 nights, arriving 20 October (not a holiday). Cancelled at 10:00 on 19 October, less than 48 hours before check-in time: the first night (¥680) is charged and **¥1,360** is refunded.

**H-11 Refund timing**
Refunds go back to the original payment method within **3–7 business days**.

**H-12 Group bookings**
A booking of **10 or more rooms** is a group contract outside this policy; hand it over to a human agent.
